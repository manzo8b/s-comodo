# Method & sources

We begin with a documented event, question or cultural practice, then study its historical context. Each comparison explains what is shared and what is not.

Dates, quotations, statistics and central factual statements must be traceable to identifiable references. The numbered notes link to a source list below each essay. We seek primary sources wherever appropriate and make uncertainty explicit.

A person or institution may offer an interested account. In that case, it is attributed as a claim rather than presented as settled fact.

**Published essays undergo human review.** Substantive corrections and subsequent updates must be differentiated. See [Corrections]({{ '/en/corrections/' | relative_url }}) and [AI use]({{ '/en/ai-use/' | relative_url }}).
