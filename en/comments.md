# Comment guidelines

Readers can discuss essays using **HTML Comment Box**, a third-party comment service. No account is required to submit a comment. Comments generally appear immediately, subject to the provider's automated spam and abuse filters.

## Rules

Disagree freely with claims and arguments. Do not post threats, harassment, discriminatory abuse, impersonation, spam or personal information belonging to others. The editor can remove inappropriate posts after publication.

Comments reflect their authors' views, **not necessarily those of S-COMODO**.

The widget loads only after you select **Show comments** beneath an essay. This contacts the service, which may process technical data and use cookies. Read our [Privacy notice]({{ '/en/privacy/' | relative_url }}) and the [provider's privacy policy](https://www.htmlcommentbox.com/static/privacy.pdf).

For factual errors in the article itself, use [Corrections]({{ '/en/corrections/' | relative_url }}) rather than the public conversation.
