# Corrections

We correct factual errors even when they do not alter an article's overall argument.

If you find a mistake, identify the article, quote the relevant passage and, when possible, provide a reliable document. Reports are reviewed against the available evidence. Significant corrections are dated and explained in the article's correction log; updates based on subsequently available information are labelled separately.

[**Report a factual error on GitHub ↗**](https://github.com/manzo8b/s-comodo/issues/new?template=correction-en.yml)

**Important:** GitHub Issues are public and require a GitHub account. Do not share private or sensitive data. This is not a channel for confidential privacy requests.
