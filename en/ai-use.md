# AI use

AI tools may assist with research organisation, early drafts, translation, accessibility tasks and original editorial illustrations.

**A language model is not a documentary source.** Factual claims require separate evidence, clear attribution and human editorial approval before publication.

AI-assisted images are identified as illustrations rather than passed off as photographs of real historic events. Published sources and relevant corrections appear alongside each article.
